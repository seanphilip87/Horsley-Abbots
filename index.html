CHAPTER ONE
A Damp Arrival in Horsley Abbots

Stetson Hinchfliffe did not enter a room; he intruded upon it. Leaning heavily on a blackthorn cane that was three inches too short, forcing an aggressive stoop. Behind his ear sat his half-eaten cheddar and margarine sandwich.

Every shadow in this village hides a secret. Every inhabitant is a liar. The environment itself is hostile.

Stetson took a bite of his sandwich. It was time to go to work.
